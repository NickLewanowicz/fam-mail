My own big-system-text setting was not something I could turn on — no browser was handed to me tonight, so I walked the evidence pack: 3 screenshots (baseline, r1, r3), the measured audit JSON, and the four blueprint files the Maintainer allowed me (AppShell, WizardActionBar, index.css, PostcardPreview).

## How I walked tonight (read this first)

The app was awake but nobody gave me hands. I could not click, type, or mis-tap anything myself. Every step below is traced through the evidence pack and marked:

- **VERIFIED** — I saw it in a screenshot, or the measuring machine reported it (JSON), or it is written plainly in a blueprint file I was allowed.
- **LIKELY** — measured fact plus my own reasoning about how my phone behaves.
- **COULD-NOT-CHECK** — I could not see it and will not pretend I did.

I never signed in with real credentials and I never reached a real send. In fact I *stopped* before Send — see F1. That stop is the honest result of this walkthrough.

## What felt good for someone like me

- **The sticky thumb bar is the kindest thing in the pack.** On the wizard, "← Back" sits bottom-left and the next-step button ("Write Message →") sits bottom-right, on a bar that never scrolls away. That is exactly where my right thumb rests. Back on the left reads like a book — go back where you came from. **VERIFIED** — `nightshift-r3-mobile-wizard-actionbar.png` and `WizardActionBar.tsx` (h-16 fixed bar, both buttons at least 44px tall, padded for the phone's bottom edge so it isn't glued to the very last pixel of the glass).
- **The whole picture frame is the button.** The upload step gives me one big dashed frame — "Upload your photo" — with a picture icon. Nothing tiny to aim at. **VERIFIED** — r3 screenshot; and `PostcardPreview.tsx` makes the entire card front tappable on the photo step, with Enter/Space bridged too.
- **My shaky double-taps won't zoom the page.** The blueprint turns off the double-tap zoom and the orange flash on every tap for buttons and links (`index.css`, `touch-action: manipulation`, tap-highlight removed). I never get trapped zooming when I only meant to tap twice. **VERIFIED** — code.
- **If my phone asks for less motion, the card calms down.** The flip becomes a plain crossfade instead of a somersault, and every animation collapses to almost nothing (`index.css` reduced-motion belt; `PostcardPreview.tsx` crossfade; Flip control is a full 44px target). **VERIFIED** — code.
- **The boxes I type in stay big.** Every input is floored at 16px on phone widths — no pinch to type, no jump-zoom when I tap a field. The measuring machine agrees: **0 undersized inputs on all five measured pages**. **VERIFIED** — `ux-audit-results-mobile.json` (`inputsUnder16px: 0` everywhere) and `index.css`.
- **My big camera photos get shrunk politely, not thrown back.** Before upload, phone-sized photos are compressed automatically instead of bouncing with an error. **VERIFIED** — `PostcardPreview.tsx`.
- **Mistakes on the photo get plain words and a real Dismiss button.** "Image must be under 10MB" with a Dismiss I can actually tap (44px). **VERIFIED** — code.
- **The sideways mess from before is gone.** In the baseline picture something was clipped off the right edge of the header; by r1 the avatar circle sits whole. **VERIFIED** — compare `nightshift-baseline-mobile-review.png` with `nightshift-r1-mobile-review.png`.
- **Three plain words at the bottom of every ordinary page: Home, Create, History.** Finger-height targets, and the current page is marked for screen readers too. **VERIFIED in the blueprint** (`AppShell.tsx`) — but see F-note: no screenshot of the rebuilt tab bar was in my pack, so I never laid eyes on it.

## What would scare or confuse me

The new phone header hides it (`AppShell.tsx`: the badge is `hidden sm:flex` — phones get nothing, with a note claiming the Review page shows a banner above Send instead). My newest Review screenshot shows **no banner above Send** — though that shot is from the *older* round, so maybe the banner came later. I could not confirm. What I can confirm: on my phone, in everything I was shown, there is no visible "this is practice" anywhere. I stopped at Send. **VERIFIED** (badge hidden on phones; absent in r1/r3 screenshots) / **COULD-NOT-CHECK** (whether the rebuilt Review banner exists).
- **Nobody tells me what a card costs.** Three sizes to choose — 6×4″, 9×6″, 11×6″ — with no prices next to any of them, and the Send button says nothing about money or who gets the card. For fifty years I knew what a stamp cost before I licked it. **VERIFIED** — `nightshift-r1-mobile-review.png`.
- **Keyboard gibberish under the Send button:** "⌘/Ctrl+Enter to send". There is no Ctrl on my phone. If the button needs a secret knock, I don't know it. **VERIFIED** — r1 screenshot.
- **Mouse words on my thumb's screen:** "or drag and drop" under Upload, and "Click to write..." on the card. Drag with what? My thumb doesn't drag, it taps. **VERIFIED** — r3 screenshot and `PostcardPreview.tsx`.
- **Five circles with numbers and no names.** The wizard shows ✓, 2, 3, 4, 5 with connecting lines. Two of what? I can't plan a trip when the signposts are blank. **VERIFIED** — r3 screenshot.
- **Tiny print that will not grow:** my own words on the card preview render at a fixed 12px, tab labels are pinned at 11px, the photo-name chip 10px, and "BARCODE ZONE" is 7px red slivers I could barely see in the old screenshot. The preview is a miniature of the real card, so I forgive its smallness *a little* — but I'd pinch to proofread my words before mailing them, and I never get small-print credit. **VERIFIED** — `PostcardPreview.tsx`, `AppShell.tsx`.

## Friction log

### F1 · The pretend-mode badge disappeared from my phone — friction · severity 3
- where: header on every phone page, and above Send on Review
- what: Baseline header showed a "MOCK" tag. The rebuild hides it on phones (`AppShell.tsx`: `hidden sm:flex` on the mode badge — comment says Review now carries a banner above Send). My newest Review screenshot shows no such banner, but it predates the rebuild; I could not verify the banner exists now. Net: in everything I was shown, a 74-year-old on a phone has no visible proof of practice-vs-real.
- quote: "> Before, there was a little tag that said MOCK — I decided it meant practice, like a mock fight. Now it's gone, and above Send there's nothing telling me whether this card is real or pretend. I put my hand on that button and I stopped."
- expectation violated: I should see, on my phone, before Send — whether nothing (or something real) will be mailed. Evidence: `nightshift-baseline-mobile-review.png` (badge present) vs `nightshift-r1-mobile-review.png` + `nightshift-r3-mobile-wizard-actionbar.png` (absent); `AppShell.tsx`. If the rebuilt Review truly has the banner, this drops to a 2 (nav hiding only); if it doesn't, this is a 4 at the money moment.
- **VERIFIED** (hiding + absence in screenshots) / **COULD-NOT-CHECK** (current Review banner)

### F2 · No price anywhere before Send — friction · severity 3
- where: Review step, size picker
- what: 6×4″ / 9×6″ / 11×6″ buttons carry no prices; Send says no amount, no "this mails a real card to Eleanor for about $X". The money moment is not unmistakable in my evidence.
- quote: "> I always knew what a stamp cost before I'd lick one. Here I'm asked to pick a size and push a black button, and not once does anybody say what it'll cost or that it goes to a real mailbox."
- expectation violated: The send step must say what, to whom, roughly how much. Evidence: `nightshift-r1-mobile-review.png`. **VERIFIED** in that screenshot; **LIKELY** unchanged (nothing in the pack shows a price was added).

### F3 · My ZIP keyboard will be the alphabet — friction · severity 2

## Forgiveness findings

- **Wrong photo:** Forgiving by blueprint — a 44px × removes it, tapping the photo reopens the picker, and my words live separately so they shouldn't be lost. **LIKELY** forgiving; never exercised. **COULD-NOT-CHECK** live.
- **Back out of Review/steps:** A thumb-height Back sits bottom-left on every wizard step, and the page always reopens at the top — predictable. Whether photo + words survive the round trip was not in my pack. **COULD-NOT-CHECK**.
- **Double-tap Send:** A spinner labeled "Sending..." appears (**VERIFIED**), but the hard lock against a second send is **COULD-NOT-CHECK**. I never got to try — see F1: I stopped at the money moment because no screen on my phone would tell me practice-vs-real.
- **Interruption (phone call mid-wizard):** No evidence in the pack. **COULD-NOT-CHECK** — and this is the test I most wanted to pass for the rebuild.
- **Mode indicator before finishing a flow:** NOT VISIBLE on my phone in any evidence I was shown (**VERIFIED** absence). Per my own rules I stopped before Send. This is the single biggest acceptance gap in the pack.

## Top-3 asks

1. **Tell me the truth above the Send button, on my phone, in big words: "This is practice — nothing will be mailed," or, when it's real, "This mails a real card to Eleanor for about a dollar."** Bring back the pretend-mode badge for phones too. I put my hand on that button tonight and nothing on my screen told me those three things, so I took my hand off.
2. **When I tap the ZIP box, give me the number keys.** All twelve boxes on the address page should ask for the right keyboard — and the price belongs next to the three sizes, so I know what I'm choosing between.
3. **Talk thumb to me.** "Or tap to choose a picture" instead of "drag and drop"; "Tap to write" instead of "Click"; and give my five little circles their names — Photo, Message, Address, and whatever the last two are called — so I always know which room of the card I'm standing in.
